01 / THE IDEA

A familiar gesture.
A different kind of money.

fork2checkbook explores a digital note that can leave its original dock. The physical handoff works without a phone, QR code, dock, or Internet. Funding and eventual redemption remain real RGB transitions settled later on Mutinynet.

↔

Offline possession

A 603-byte bearer capability moves between bills through acknowledged BLE chunks and survives a reboot.

◎

Unknown final wallet

The holder's chosen dock creates its invoice only at redemption. It was not known when the disposable note was funded.

✓

Exact authorization

Each spend has a separate immutable plan and exact one-use human confirmation. Unknown outcomes never retry automatically.

05 / HONEST BOUNDARIES

A working prototype.
Not magic cash.

The offline experience is real, but its trust assumptions are part of the result—not footnotes hidden from the demo. We target minimal identity disclosure and cash-like interchangeability, not proven cryptographic anonymity or strict fungibility.

⌂

Trusted vault

The vault retains note wallet N's keys and can censor or spend an active note.

⧉

Copy risk

A copied capsule is first-redemption-wins. Offline double spending is detected later, not prevented.

△

Still to integrate

QVAC routes guarded note actions locally, but its fixed adversarial evaluation is open. WDK is not integrated. Mutinynet has no real dollar value.
